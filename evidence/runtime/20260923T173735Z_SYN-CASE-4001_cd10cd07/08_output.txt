We reviewed your June and July 2026 bills and the associated meter readings. The July bill is higher because the meter showed higher measured usage in July than in June.

What we reviewed
We reviewed the June 2026 and July 2026 billing records, the actual meter reads taken on 2026-06-30 and 2026-07-31, the supplied rate components (energy charge and fixed charge), the remote meter diagnostic from 2026-07-28, and the usage history provided for recent months.

What we found
The meter register increased from 18,440 kWh on 2026-06-30 to 19,160 kWh on 2026-07-31, which corresponds to 480 kWh for June and 720 kWh for July. The July consumption is 240 kWh (50%) higher than June. The supplied energy rate and fixed charge reproduce the billed totals for both months. The remote diagnostic returned PASS and there are no recorded meter events in the supplied records.

Why your bill changed
The billed amount increased because measured energy use increased: July usage (720 kWh) is 240 kWh higher than June (480 kWh). Using the supplied rate (0.21 USD per kWh) and fixed charge (10.00 USD), the difference in charges equals 240 kWh × 0.21 USD/kWh = 50.40 USD, which matches the increase in the July bill.

What happens next
If you would like, we can provide guidance on checking household usage (for example, reviewing air conditioning, appliance use, or occupancy changes) and request additional documents such as a full bill line-item breakdown or a 12-month usage history for comparison.
